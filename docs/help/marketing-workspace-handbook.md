# Showwork Marketing Workspace handbook

Reviewed: 27 October 2026.

Web version: `/help/workspace`. Guides describe the implemented interface; options depend on your role, plan and connected-platform permissions. This document does not certify live provider integrations.

## Getting started

Find your way around and set up your first workspace.

### Create your first Marketing Workspace

Give each business or client a dedicated home for content, knowledge and conversations.

Guide: /help/workspace?guide=create-workspace

**Before you begin:** An account with workspace allowance available.

1. Sign in to Showwork and open Marketing Workspaces from your dashboard.
2. Use the new-workspace form and enter the business or client name.
3. Select Create workspace and wait for the new workspace to open. A private client password is created automatically.
4. Start with Knowledge to add business information, then Channels to connect social accounts.
5. Invite your team from People when you are ready to collaborate.

**What happens next:** A separate workspace for this business, with its own content, sources, access and connections.

**Troubleshooting**

- If creation is unavailable, check your workspace allowance and subscription on Billing.

Related: Find tools and navigate the workspace; Upload business documents; Invite a teammate and choose their role.

### Find tools and navigate the workspace

Use desktop navigation, mobile menus and the overview without losing your place.

Guide: /help/workspace?guide=navigate-workspace

**Before you begin:** Any workspace member; available sections depend on permissions.

1. Open a workspace and use Overview to see planning activity, upcoming work and shortcuts.
2. On desktop, select a section from the left menu. Use its chevron control to collapse the menu to icons or expand it again.
3. On mobile, use Home, Content, Inbox and AI Studio in the bottom menu. Open More for the remaining sections available to you.
4. Use the blue workspace header to open Client view or Settings when available.
5. Use Help & guides from the menu to open these instructions in a separate tab.

**What happens next:** You can reach the tools your role permits and keep the workspace open while reading help.

**Good to know:** Switching sections preserves visited panels. Save edits before leaving the website or refreshing the browser.

Related: Find posts by date, platform or workflow stage; Grant internal review or customize feature access; Share the private client portal.

## Content planning

Create, organize and prepare content for your team.

### Create a content-calendar post

Plan a post manually with its creative brief, channel and publishing time.

Guide: /help/workspace?guide=create-post

**Before you begin:** Plan and edit content permission.

1. Open Content and choose Add post on the date you want to use.
2. Choose the platform and content format, and check the post date and time.
3. Fill in the content idea and relevant fields, including hook, objective, target audience, caption, creative direction, call to action and hashtags where shown.
4. Review platform-specific fields and any additional custom details before saving.
5. Save the post, then open its calendar card to check the brief and workflow stage.

**What happens next:** The saved post is available to your team. It must pass internal review before appearing in the client portal.

**Good to know:** Saving a plan does not publish it. Approval, media, account access and publishing settings are separate requirements.

Related: Edit a post and its creative brief; Upload creative assets to a post; Give internal feedback and approve for client review; Turn automatic publishing on or off.

### Edit a post and its creative brief

Update the words and instructions your team uses to produce content.

Guide: /help/workspace?guide=edit-post

**Before you begin:** Plan and edit content permission; approved content may be locked.

1. Open Content and select the post card.
2. Use the Content tab to review the brief and creative assets.
3. Use the edit controls provided for the post details or brief. Update the fields you need rather than replacing the entire plan.
4. Use Reimagine on an individual field where available if you want an AI-assisted alternative. Review its accuracy before keeping it.
5. Save your changes and reopen the post to confirm the saved version.

**What happens next:** The team works from the updated brief.

**Troubleshooting**

- Client-approved content is protected from ordinary editing. Check its review state and available return-to-review controls before making changes.
- AI tools require the appropriate permission and available AI access.

Related: Upload creative assets to a post; Give internal feedback and approve for client review; Generate a content batch with AI Studio.

### Upload creative assets to a post

Attach the actual images or videos your team will review and publish.

Guide: /help/workspace?guide=attach-creatives

**Before you begin:** Upload creatives permission; post must allow changes.

1. Open the post from Content.
2. In the Content tab, find the creative upload controls and select the files from your device.
3. Wait for the upload to complete. Keep the page open while progress is displayed.
4. Review the uploaded media in the post, including its order and platform suitability.
5. Ask an internal reviewer to review the final creative and caption together.

**What happens next:** The post has media attached for review and, where supported, publishing.

**Good to know:** An attached file is not proof that a platform will accept it. Media format, duration and account-specific rules are checked separately.

Related: Give internal feedback and approve for client review; Understand Approved, Scheduled, Published and Failed; Edit a post and its creative brief.

### Inspect attached images, videos and timed feedback

Check the creative itself rather than reviewing only the caption.

Guide: /help/workspace?guide=review-media

**Before you begin:** View calendar permission; media must be attached to the post.

1. Open the post from Content and select the Content tab.
2. Choose an attached image or video using the media thumbnails when there are several assets.
3. Use the video player controls to inspect playback where applicable.
4. If timed video comments are present, select a comment timestamp to jump to that moment.
5. Use Internal review for private team feedback or Client review to read the client decision and feedback.

**What happens next:** You can review the actual asset alongside the brief and relevant feedback.

**Good to know:** Viewing a media comment does not grant permission to write internal feedback or approve content. Those abilities are controlled separately.

Related: Upload creative assets to a post; Give internal feedback and approve for client review; Get client approval or respond to revisions.

### Import an existing content calendar

Bring a prepared plan into Showwork and correct ambiguous entries before saving.

Guide: /help/workspace?guide=import-calendar

**Before you begin:** Plan and edit content permission.

1. Open Content and select Import calendar.
2. Choose Browse files and select PDF, CSV, Excel, Word, TXT or HTML. The current importer accepts files up to 3 MB and up to 200 items. Wait for Showwork to open the file.
3. Review Import preferences: timezone, numeric date order, missing year, default time, default platform and time notation.
4. Check Column mappings. Keep useful custom fields and ignore columns that should not become content.
5. Review the content preview. Resolve entries needing attention, inspect duplicates, and select the posts to keep.
6. Select Import and wait for confirmation, then choose Go to calendar.

**What happens next:** Only the selected, validated entries are added to the calendar.

**Troubleshooting**

- Do not guess ambiguous dates: set the date order or correct the preview.
- If confirmation fails, use Retry confirmation where offered and inspect the calendar before importing again.

Related: Create a content-calendar post; Find posts by date, platform or workflow stage; Delete a post or reset a content calendar.

4. Select a post to inspect its delivery-style workflow and detailed status.

**What happens next:** You see the relevant posts without changing their actual workflow state.

**Good to know:** Approved means review approval. Scheduled and Published are separate publishing states.

Keep production information that does not fit the standard brief fields.

1. Open the Add post form and complete the required planning fields.
2. Find the custom-details area and select Add a custom detail.

5. Open the saved post to review its additional details with the rest of the brief.

**What happens next:** Your post carries the additional information the team needs.

**Good to know:** Custom fields are planning details. Do not assume they become a social caption, an automatic workflow action or a publishing instruction.

### Delete a post or reset a content calendar

Understand the difference between removing one post and clearing the plan.

Guide: /help/workspace?guide=delete-reset

**Before you begin:** The relevant delete permission; Reset is owner-only.

1. For a single post, locate its card in Content and use Delete post. Read and confirm the deletion prompt.
2. For a full calendar reset, use Reset in the Content toolbar only if you intend to clear the plan.
3. Read the reset confirmation and complete the required confirmation before continuing.
4. After the action finishes, check the calendar and any affected publishing status.

**What happens next:** The selected deletion or reset is applied, rather than merely hiding posts with filters.

**Good to know:** These are destructive actions. Keep a copy of information you need before confirming; do not use Reset to troubleshoot a filter or loading issue.

## Reviews & approvals

Move work from private team review to client approval.

### Give internal feedback and approve for client review

Keep unfinished work private until an authorized reviewer clears it.

Guide: /help/workspace?guide=internal-review

**Before you begin:** Internal review permission. Owners always have it; managers have it by default unless changed.

1. Open the post from Content and select Internal review.
2. Review the brief, caption and attached media.
3. Enter Private team feedback and choose Share internal feedback if changes are needed.
4. When the content is ready, select Approve for client review.
5. Check that the stage now shows internal approval before asking the client to review.

**What happens next:** Internally approved posts can appear in the unlocked client portal. Internal feedback stays private to the team.

**Good to know:** Internal feedback and approval notify the relevant workspace content team by email. Delivery depends on the email service; these messages are not sent to clients as internal feedback.

**Troubleshooting**

- If feedback and approval controls are unavailable, ask the owner to check your Internal review permission in People.

Related: Get client approval or respond to revisions; Grant internal review or customize feature access; Share the private client portal.

### Get client approval or respond to revisions

Share work after internal approval and follow the client decision.

Guide: /help/workspace?guide=client-review

**Before you begin:** Client portal access for the client; relevant content permissions for the team.

1. Finish internal review for the post first.
2. Open Client to find the private client-view link and password. Share them with the intended reviewer.
3. The client opens the link, enters the password and selects an available post to review.
4. The client uses the review controls to approve the post or provide feedback and request a revision.
5. Back in the workspace, open the post’s Client review tab to read the decision and feedback. Update the work using the controls available for its state.
6. Check Publishing separately when approval is complete.

**What happens next:** The client’s feedback or approval is recorded on the post.

**Good to know:** Before internal approval, the post is hidden from the client. Client approval alone does not guarantee that publishing is scheduled or successful.

Related: Give internal feedback and approve for client review; Turn automatic publishing on or off; Understand Approved, Scheduled, Published and Failed.

### Return a post to internal review

Bring work back into the private team workflow when it needs another check.

Guide: /help/workspace?guide=reopen-internal-review

**Before you begin:** Internal review permission; the post must allow reopening.

1. Open the post and choose Internal review.
2. Check its current internal and client approval states.
3. Use Return to internal review when the control is available.
4. Add clear private feedback explaining what needs to change.
5. Complete the internal review again before sending the updated work for client review.

**What happens next:** The post returns to the internal workflow and must be cleared again before client review.

**Troubleshooting**

- Client-approved content may be locked. If the control is unavailable, check the post’s protected state rather than deleting and recreating it.

Related: Give internal feedback and approve for client review; Get client approval or respond to revisions.

## Business Knowledge

Give AI the facts and personality behind your brand.

### Upload business documents

Give content generation and customer replies a factual foundation.

Guide: /help/workspace?guide=upload-knowledge

**Before you begin:** Manage business knowledge permission and available AI access.

1. Open Knowledge in AI Studio.
2. Select Upload documents and choose one or several PDF, Word, Excel, CSV or text files accepted by the uploader.
3. Wait for uploading and reading to finish. Follow any displayed size, format or extraction errors.
4. Check that each successful file appears in Documents & websites.
5. Review the source-derived business overview and the indexing status before generating new work.

**What happens next:** Successfully processed sources are saved against this workspace and can inform its AI context.

**Good to know:** Reading a document and using it in a particular answer are different steps. AI retrieves relevant information within context limits; it does not paste every document into every request. Scanned or unreadable files may need a text-readable replacement.

Related: Add a website to Business Knowledge; Set your Brand Personality; Keep knowledge accurate and remove outdated sources; Generate a content batch with AI Studio.

**Good to know:** The current reader visits the homepage and up to four relevant pages, not the entire website. Login-only, blocked and JavaScript-only content may be unavailable.

**What happens next:** The saved identity can shape AI Studio content and customer replies for this workspace.

**Good to know:** Brand voice does not override facts, the customer’s intent or handoff and safety rules. If no personality is saved, existing generation continues normally.

## AI Studio

Brief, generate and refine content before it reaches the calendar.

### Generate a content batch with AI Studio

Turn a clear brief and business knowledge into editable draft content.

Guide: /help/workspace?guide=generate-content

**Before you begin:** Generate AI content permission, available AI allowance and required workspace access.

1. Add current business sources in Knowledge, then open Generate.
2. Choose a campaign objective, or let the strategy follow your business knowledge.
3. Set the date range, cadence, timezone and platform schedule using the available rhythm controls.
4. Select the platforms you want content for.
5. Use the creative-direction instructions to describe the audience need, campaign angle, priorities and exclusions. Review the batch summary.
6. Start generation and follow its progress. When drafts are ready, open the draft review before confirming content.

**What happens next:** AI creates a draft batch for human review; it does not directly publish the content.

**Good to know:** AI requests consume your configured allowance. Generation time varies with batch size and service availability; there is no guaranteed instant completion.

**Troubleshooting**

- If a request fails, check whether drafts already exist before retrying so you do not create another batch unnecessarily.

Related: Review, edit and confirm AI drafts; Answer AI clarification questions; Set your Brand Personality; Turn a Randy conversation into a content prompt.

### Review, edit and confirm AI drafts

Make generated content accurate and useful before moving it into the team workflow.

Guide: /help/workspace?guide=review-ai-drafts

**Before you begin:** Generate AI content and applicable content-editing access.

1. Open the draft review after generation, or resume the available saved-drafts review.
2. Move between drafts and check the idea, hook, objective, target audience, caption and creative direction.
3. Correct factual claims, pricing, dates, promises and any details that do not match your business knowledge.
4. Edit or remove drafts that are unsuitable. Confirm the drafts you want to keep using the provided confirmation controls.
5. Open Content and review the confirmed posts before producing assets and requesting internal approval.

**What happens next:** Confirmed drafts move into normal content planning; internal and client approval still follow.

**Good to know:** Confirm all drafts is a bulk action. Read the batch first; confirmation is not the same as approval or publishing.

Related: Generate a content batch with AI Studio; Edit a post and its creative brief; Give internal feedback and approve for client review.

### Answer AI clarification questions

Supply important missing facts instead of asking AI to invent them.

Guide: /help/workspace?guide=missing-information

**Before you begin:** Generate AI content permission; saving lasting knowledge requires knowledge-management access.

1. Read any clarification request shown during generation.
2. Enter specific factual answers in the fields provided, such as the intended audience, offer or campaign goal.
3. If you want to reuse the answers and have permission, select Save answers to business knowledge and wait for confirmation.
4. Select Use answers for this request, or Continue without unresolved details when you want unsupported details omitted. If sources are still processing, you can wait and retry instead.
5. For facts that should be reused, also maintain an authoritative source in Knowledge.

**What happens next:** The current brief has better factual context for generation.

**Good to know:** Do not assume that every message or one-off instruction becomes permanent knowledge. Check the save confirmation and your source library.

Related: Generate a content batch with AI Studio; Upload business documents; Keep knowledge accurate and remove outdated sources.

## Randy

Chat about your business, research ideas and build your knowledge.

### Chat and research with Randy

Talk about your business, explore sourced ideas and propose additions to Business Knowledge.

Guide: /help/workspace?guide=research-question

**Before you begin:** Knowledge view permission to read your conversations; AI generation permission and active workspace to chat. Knowledge management permission to research or confirm additions.

1. Open Randy in AI Studio. Select Start conversation or choose one of your saved conversations.
2. Ask about your business, audience, brand or known rules. Randy uses relevant Business Knowledge passages and recent conversation; it may ask for missing details.
3. To investigate online, ask Randy to research a specific question. Review the proposed public research topic, then select Start research. Keep private customer details out of the topic.
4. Read the findings in the conversation and expand Sources to inspect supporting evidence. Continue asking follow-up questions in the same conversation.
5. Randy may offer a useful knowledge addition, or you can ask for one. Review the proposed addition and select Confirm & save if accurate. It adds a shared source; existing conflicting documents are not overwritten.
6. To create content from the discussion, select Create content prompt or ask Randy. Review the prompt in chat, then Copy prompt or Use in Generate.

**What happens next:** Your conversation is saved privately to your login within this workspace. Confirmed additions become shared Business Knowledge sources.

**Good to know:** Randy does not read every document in every reply: it retrieves relevant passages. Research is external evidence, not company policy. Nothing is saved to knowledge without confirmation. No content is automatically generated or published.

Related: Check research evidence in your conversation; Turn a Randy conversation into a content prompt; Add a website to Business Knowledge.

### Check research evidence in your conversation

Inspect sources, discuss uncertainty and challenge findings without leaving Randy.

Guide: /help/workspace?guide=research-feedback

**Before you begin:** Knowledge view to read your own conversations; AI generation permission to continue chatting.

1. Open Randy and choose the conversation containing your research.
2. Expand Sources beneath the answer and open supporting links to inspect the evidence.
3. Ask Randy which parts are verified, uncertain or proposed experiments, and explain any incorrect assumptions.
4. Ask a follow-up question or request more research when the evidence is insufficient.
5. Review any proposed knowledge addition separately. Nothing is saved as business information until an authorized member confirms it.

**What happens next:** Research can inform your discussion while keeping uncertainty visible.

**Good to know:** Chat feedback does not automatically edit or delete historical research records or retract existing generated content. External research must not be treated as proof of company prices, policies or capabilities.

Related: Chat and research with Randy; Turn a Randy conversation into a content prompt; Keep knowledge accurate and remove outdated sources.

### Turn a Randy conversation into a content prompt

Prepare a generation prompt directly from your business discussion and research.

Guide: /help/workspace?guide=research-brief

**Before you begin:** AI generation permission to prepare a prompt and use Generate.

1. Discuss the idea or research findings with Randy in the same conversation.
2. Ask to create content from the discussion, or select Create content prompt below the latest answer.
3. Review the content prompt Randy prepares. Edit the prompt in its card to refine the objective, audience, angle and creative direction.
4. Select Copy prompt to copy the text, or Use in Generate to open Generate with the instructions filled in.
5. Review your platforms, dates and other generation settings, then start generation when ready.

**What happens next:** A reviewed prompt carries your discussion into AI content generation.

**Good to know:** Use in Generate does not automatically generate, approve or publish posts. Knowledge additions are separate and require confirmation.

Related: Generate a content batch with AI Studio; Check research evidence in your conversation; Review, edit and confirm AI drafts.

## Publishing

Control scheduling, authorizations and publishing status.

### Turn automatic publishing on or off

Choose whether new client approvals should enter the publishing queue.

Guide: /help/workspace?guide=auto-publish

**Before you begin:** Publishing-management permission.

1. Open Content and locate Auto-publish approved posts.
2. Read the current On or Off state and open How publishing works if needed.
3. Use the switch and wait for the setting to save.
4. With it on, new client approvals can be queued for their calendar time on supported connected channels when all requirements are met.
5. With it off, client approval stays an approval; scheduled posts are paused according to the setting.

**What happens next:** The workspace uses your chosen automatic-publishing behaviour.

**Good to know:** Turning it on does not queue posts approved while it was off. Deliveries already started may finish. TikTok also needs per-post settings and consent.

Related: Get client approval or respond to revisions; Authorize a TikTok post for publishing; Understand Approved, Scheduled, Published and Failed.

### Schedule or publish an approved post

Check the final requirements and use the available publishing controls.

Guide: /help/workspace?guide=publish-post

**Before you begin:** Publishing-management permission and the correct connected channel.

1. Open the post and confirm both internal and client approval are complete.
2. Check the attached media, caption, selected account and scheduled date/time.
3. Open Publishing in the post details.
4. Use the scheduling or publish-now action offered for that platform, completing any additional settings or authorization.
5. Watch the publishing state and inspect the result or error after processing.

**What happens next:** The post is scheduled or submitted to the provider; Published is shown only after the publishing flow confirms it.

**Good to know:** Scheduling is not proof of delivery. Provider validation, expired tokens, rate limits and media restrictions can stop a post.

Related: Turn automatic publishing on or off; Authorize a TikTok post for publishing; Understand Approved, Scheduled, Published and Failed.

### Authorize a TikTok post for publishing

Set visibility and consent for each TikTok post.

Guide: /help/workspace?guide=tiktok-publish

**Before you begin:** Publishing-management permission, TikTok connection and both review approvals.

1. Open the post’s Publishing tab and review TikTok settings.
2. Choose an available privacy level and review the account-specific media and interaction options.
3. Set commercial-content disclosures and AI-generated-media labels accurately when applicable.
4. After approval, review TikTok’s displayed terms and give publishing consent.
5. Select Authorize and schedule or Authorize and publish now.
6. For a failed attempt, check TikTok first and use the confirmation and retry controls only if it did not publish.

**What happens next:** The specific post has the required authorization for the selected publishing action.

**Good to know:** TikTok’s available privacy levels and publishing eligibility come from the connected account and application access; they are not guaranteed by a connection badge.

Related: Connect or reconnect TikTok; Turn automatic publishing on or off; Understand Approved, Scheduled, Published and Failed.

### Understand Approved, Scheduled, Published and Failed

Separate review decisions from actual social-platform delivery.

Guide: /help/workspace?guide=publishing-status

**Before you begin:** View calendar; publishing access for corrective actions.

1. Open the post and inspect its workflow and Publishing tab.
2. If it is pending internal review, complete that review before requesting client approval.
3. If it is Approved, check auto-publishing, channel access, media requirements and any per-post authorization.
4. If it is Scheduled, check the scheduled time and allow the worker/provider to process it.
5. If it is Failed, read the error, check the provider for an existing post, correct the issue and use an offered retry action.

**What happens next:** You know which requirement or publishing stage needs attention.

**Troubleshooting**
