# Showwork Portfolio handbook

Reviewed: 27 October 2026.

Web version: `/help/portfolio`. Guides describe the implemented interface; options depend on your role, plan and connected-platform permissions. This document does not certify live provider integrations.

## Getting started

Create a public home for your best work.

### Create your portfolio

Set up the public page that introduces your work and makes it easy to contact you.

Guide: /help/portfolio?guide=create-portfolio

**Before you begin:** A signed-in creator account without an existing personal portfolio; agency creation follows its own flow.

1. Open Portfolio from your dashboard.
2. Enter your company or brand name and a short tagline.
3. Add your contact email and WhatsApp number if you want visitors to reach you through those channels.
4. Choose the call-to-action text and submit the creation form.
5. Open the portfolio studio and continue with Identity and Work before sharing the page widely.

**What happens next:** Your portfolio record is created and you can build its public presentation.

**Good to know:** Portfolio is a public showcase, not a private project delivery. Do not upload client-confidential material unless you are authorized to make it public.

Related: Find your way around Portfolio Studio; Update your name and tagline; Add a portfolio work section; Preview and share the public portfolio.

### Find your way around Portfolio Studio

Know which section controls your identity, work and social proof.

Guide: /help/portfolio?guide=navigate-portfolio

**Before you begin:** The portfolio owner.

1. Open Portfolio in your dashboard and select the intended portfolio if you have an agency collection.
2. Use Overview to see file, section, document and testimonial counts and the setup checklist.
3. Open Identity to manage your introduction, banners and contact information.
4. Open Work for media sections and Testimonials for client stories.
5. Use Preview live site to inspect the visitor experience in another tab. On mobile, use the studio navigation available above the content.

**What happens next:** You can reach the correct editing area without confusing the studio with the public site.

Related: Update your name and tagline; Add a portfolio work section; Approve, hide or delete testimonials.

### Prepare a portfolio before sharing it

Use the overview checklist as a practical launch review.

Guide: /help/portfolio?guide=portfolio-checklist

**Before you begin:** The portfolio owner.

1. Open Overview and inspect the identity, work-section, client-social-proof and live-status checklist.
2. Save an accurate name, useful tagline and working contact information in Identity.
3. Add at least one populated work section and choose a representative cover.
4. Review any testimonials you intend to publish.
5. Open Preview live site and check the desktop and mobile presentation before sending the link.

**What happens next:** The public page has meaningful content and usable contact paths.

**Good to know:** The checklist is a setup aid. It does not certify legal permission to publish media or prove that every external link works.

Related: Set separate desktop and mobile banners; Set your email, WhatsApp and call to action; Preview and share the public portfolio.

## Identity & presentation

Shape your introduction, banners and contact details.

### Update your name and tagline

Make the first impression clear and recognizable.

Guide: /help/portfolio?guide=portfolio-identity

**Before you begin:** The portfolio owner.

1. Open Portfolio Studio and select Identity.
2. Update Company / brand name and Tagline.
3. Check that the wording explains what you do rather than only repeating your name.
4. Select Save changes and wait for the saved confirmation.
5. Open Preview live site and check the updated introduction.

**What happens next:** The saved identity fields appear on the portfolio.

**Good to know:** Changing the displayed company name is not the same as changing the public URL slug. Copy the actual current portfolio link when sharing.

Related: Add your introduction and profile photo; Set separate desktop and mobile banners; Preview and share the public portfolio.

### Set separate desktop and mobile banners

Choose media suited to wide screens and phones.

Guide: /help/portfolio?guide=portfolio-banners

**Before you begin:** The portfolio owner with the banner upload controls.

1. Open Identity and find Your portfolio’s banner.
2. Use Desktop banner to choose suitable wide-screen media.
3. Use Mobile banner to choose a version that works in a taller phone layout.
4. Wait for each upload to finish, then select Save changes.
5. Preview both screen layouts and check the crop, subject placement and readability of the introduction.

**What happens next:** Visitors receive the banner version intended for their screen.

**Good to know:** Dedicated banners take priority over the older gallery-media hero selection. Banner images are resized for presentation; this does not replace gallery originals.

Related: Choose existing gallery media as the hero; Update your name and tagline; Check the portfolio on a phone.

### Choose existing gallery media as the hero

Reuse an uploaded portfolio asset when no dedicated banner is set.

Guide: /help/portfolio?guide=portfolio-gallery-hero

**Before you begin:** The portfolio owner with suitable gallery media.

1. Upload the image or video you want to use through Work first.
2. Open Identity and locate the existing-media banner choices when available.
3. Select the desired gallery asset.
4. Save changes and open the live preview.
5. If the selected asset is not displayed as expected, check whether dedicated desktop/mobile banners are taking priority.

**What happens next:** The selected gallery hero is used when the dedicated-banner settings do not override it.

**Good to know:** Selecting gallery media does not create a different upload or change a section cover. Each is a separate presentation choice.

Related: Set separate desktop and mobile banners; Choose a section cover; Add files to an existing section.

### Add your introduction and profile photo

Tell visitors who you are and what you bring to a project.

Guide: /help/portfolio?guide=portfolio-bio

**Before you begin:** The portfolio owner.

1. Open Identity and find Introduce yourself.
2. Upload a suitable profile/bio photo using the photo control.
3. Write the short introduction in the bio field.
4. Add skills and an optional factual stat using the provided controls.
5. Save changes and inspect the public introduction section.

**What happens next:** The portfolio includes a more personal introduction alongside your work.

**Good to know:** The optional stat is text you enter, such as projects delivered. It is not an automatically verified analytics metric.

Related: Add or remove skills and an optional stat; Set your email, WhatsApp and call to action; Update your name and tagline.

### Add or remove skills and an optional stat

Summarize your strengths without overloading the introduction.

Guide: /help/portfolio?guide=portfolio-skills

**Before you begin:** The portfolio owner.

1. Open Identity and scroll to the skills field in Introduce yourself.
2. Type a skill and press Enter to add it.
3. Use the remove control on an existing skill when it is no longer relevant.
4. Enter a truthful optional stat if you want to highlight a specific achievement.
5. Save changes and review how the information reads on the public page.

**What happens next:** Your saved skills and optional achievement text appear with the introduction.

Related: Add your introduction and profile photo; Update your name and tagline.

### Set your email, WhatsApp and call to action

Give interested visitors a clear next step.

Guide: /help/portfolio?guide=portfolio-contacts

**Before you begin:** The portfolio owner.

1. Open Identity and find Contact email, WhatsApp number and Call-to-action text.
2. Enter the email address you want prospective clients to use.
3. Enter a valid WhatsApp number with the appropriate international country code.
4. Choose concise call-to-action wording and save the changes.
5. Open the public portfolio and test the email and WhatsApp actions on a suitable device.

**What happens next:** Visitors can use the configured contact actions on your public page.

**Good to know:** Portfolio WhatsApp contact links are not a WhatsApp Inbox integration. They do not connect Marketing Workspace messaging or enable automatic AI replies.

Related: Add social-profile links; Preview and share the public portfolio; Fix an email, social or WhatsApp contact link.

### Add social-profile links

Connect your public showcase to the profiles you want visitors to explore.

Guide: /help/portfolio?guide=portfolio-social-links

**Before you begin:** The portfolio owner.

1. Open Identity and find the social-link fields.
2. Enter the intended Instagram, X/Twitter, LinkedIn, TikTok, Facebook or YouTube profile addresses where applicable.
3. Check each address points to the right profile rather than an internal account-settings page.
4. Select Save changes.
5. Open the public portfolio and test the visible links.

**What happens next:** Visitors can follow the social links you supplied.

**Good to know:** These fields are public profile links, not OAuth connections. Adding them does not authorize publishing, analytics or messaging.

Related: Set your email, WhatsApp and call to action; Preview and share the public portfolio; Update your name and tagline.

## Work & media

Build sections, upload media and choose strong covers.

### Add a portfolio work section

Organize your showcase into clear collections.

Guide: /help/portfolio?guide=add-portfolio-section

**Before you begin:** The portfolio owner with available storage/upload access.

1. Open Work in Portfolio Studio.
2. Choose the add-section action.
3. Select Images, Videos, Documents or PDFs from the media choices offered.
4. Enter a clear section name and choose the files for that collection.
5. Review the selection and start the upload.
6. Wait for completion, then inspect the section in Work and on the public preview.

**What happens next:** A populated media section becomes part of the public portfolio presentation.

**Good to know:** Empty sections are not displayed as populated work on the public page. Use descriptive, visitor-friendly section names.

Related: Add files to an existing section; Choose a section cover; Rename a section.

### Add files to an existing section

Keep a collection current without building a duplicate section.

Guide: /help/portfolio?guide=upload-portfolio-media

**Before you begin:** The portfolio owner and a matching section media type.

1. Open Work and locate the existing section.
2. Use + Add files in its section controls.
3. Select files accepted by that section’s media type.
4. Start the upload and keep the page open while transfer and final registration complete.
5. Check the resulting tiles and preview the public section before retrying any missing file.

**What happens next:** The successfully registered files are added to the intended existing section.

**Good to know:** Follow the uploader’s current format, size and storage validation rather than assuming every file extension can be displayed in every browser.

Related: Recover from a failed portfolio upload; Understand video thumbnails and playback; Choose reviewable image, video and document files.

### Choose reviewable image, video and document files

Prepare work that visitors can actually open and understand.

Guide: /help/portfolio?guide=portfolio-files

**Before you begin:** The portfolio owner.

1. Choose the correct media type before selecting files for a section.
2. Follow the file picker’s accepted formats and any displayed validation errors.
3. Use browser-friendly video codecs when inline playback matters.
4. Inspect uploaded images, video playback and document previews in the public portfolio.
5. If a file cannot be viewed reliably, prepare a suitable public showcase version before uploading again.

**What happens next:** Your collections use files appropriate for the visitor experience.

**Good to know:** Upload success does not guarantee universal codec or document-preview support. Only publish work you have permission to share publicly.

Related: Add files to an existing section; Open images, videos and documents as a visitor; Troubleshoot slow thumbnails or video playback.

### Choose a section cover

Give each collection a representative first impression.

Guide: /help/portfolio?guide=portfolio-section-cover

**Before you begin:** The portfolio owner with media in the section.

1. Open Work and find the section.
2. Locate the image or video you want visitors to see as its cover.
3. Use Set as section cover on that file’s tile.
4. Wait for the change to finish.
5. Preview the public portfolio and inspect the section card or cover presentation.

**What happens next:** The section uses the chosen media as its representative cover where the layout displays one.

**Good to know:** The public page falls back to available section media when no valid chosen cover is present. A section cover is separate from the page’s hero banner.

Related: Choose existing gallery media as the hero; Add a portfolio work section; Remove a file or delete a section.

### Rename a section

Keep the public collection headings accurate as your work evolves.

Guide: /help/portfolio?guide=portfolio-rename-section

**Before you begin:** The portfolio owner.

1. Open Work and find the section heading.
2. Use the section’s rename/edit control.
3. Enter the new name and save it.
4. Check the heading in the public preview.

**What happens next:** The saved collection name appears on the portfolio.

Related: Add a portfolio work section; Choose a section cover.

### Remove a file or delete a section

Curate the public work deliberately.

Guide: /help/portfolio?guide=portfolio-delete-file

**Before you begin:** The portfolio owner with the displayed delete controls.

1. Open Work and identify the exact file or section you intend to remove.
2. For one file, choose Delete file on its tile and read the confirmation.
3. For a full section, use the section’s delete control and review what will be removed.
4. Confirm only if you no longer want that content in the portfolio.
5. Preview the page afterward and check any affected cover or hero selections.

**What happens next:** The confirmed removal is applied to the intended portfolio content.

**Good to know:** Deletion is not a temporary hiding feature. Do not assume the original can be restored from the portfolio after removal; keep your own master copy.

Related: Choose a section cover; Choose existing gallery media as the hero; Recover from a failed portfolio upload.

### Understand video thumbnails and playback

Know how saved posters help videos appear before they play.

Guide: /help/portfolio?guide=portfolio-video-posters

**Before you begin:** The portfolio owner or a public visitor.

1. Upload a supported gallery video and wait for the original transfer to finish.
2. Allow the uploader to finish its preview/poster step where shown.
3. Reload Work or the public page and check the video’s thumbnail.
4. Open the video and test actual playback separately from the poster.
5. If a preview warning appears, check the original before re-uploading; a poster failure does not necessarily mean the video upload failed.

**What happens next:** A successfully saved poster can display before the video starts.

**Good to know:** Older videos may have no saved poster, and browser codec support varies. There is no manual poster-replacement control introduced by this feature. Standalone Identity banner uploads are separate from gallery-video poster generation.

Related: Troubleshoot slow thumbnails or video playback; Recover from a failed portfolio upload; Set separate desktop and mobile banners.

## Sharing & visitor experience

Preview your public page and share the right link.

### Preview and share the public portfolio

Send visitors the public address instead of your editing dashboard.

Guide: /help/portfolio?guide=share-portfolio

**Before you begin:** The portfolio owner; the shared page is public.

1. Open Portfolio Studio and use Preview live site.
2. Inspect the introduction, sections, media, contact actions and approved testimonials.
3. Copy the public portfolio address provided by the studio.
4. Share that link wherever you want prospective clients to discover your work.
5. Open the shared address outside the editor to check the visitor experience.

**What happens next:** Visitors reach the public portfolio rather than your private editing page.

**Good to know:** The canonical portfolio address uses /portfolio/ followed by its saved slug. Portfolio sharing does not use the private client access-code gate from Project Delivery.

Related: Check the portfolio on a phone; Use the canonical link and understand older subdomains; Understand when saved changes appear publicly.

### Understand when saved changes appear publicly

Avoid looking for a delivery-style approval or publish workflow in your portfolio.

Guide: /help/portfolio?guide=portfolio-public-saves

**Before you begin:** The portfolio owner.

1. Edit Identity, Work or Testimonials using the section’s own controls.
2. Wait for the save or upload confirmation.
3. Open Preview live site or refresh the already-open public tab.
4. Check the actual saved content and testimonial visibility rather than assuming the editor’s unsaved fields are live.
5. If changes are missing, verify the selected portfolio and retry the appropriate save only after checking the result.

**What happens next:** You distinguish saved public presentation from unsaved edits.

**Good to know:** The inspected portfolio editor does not use the Marketing Workspace internal/client approval workflow. Testimonial approval is its own visibility control. Account or agency billing availability may still affect access.

Related: Update your name and tagline; Approve, hide or delete testimonials; Troubleshoot changes missing from the public page.

### Check the portfolio on a phone

Review the actual small-screen experience before sharing widely.

Guide: /help/portfolio?guide=portfolio-mobile-preview

**Before you begin:** The public portfolio link.

1. Open the public address on a phone or a mobile-size browser window.
2. Check that the mobile banner shows the subject clearly.
3. Use the visible section/menu navigation and open images, videos and documents.
4. Test contact links and the WhatsApp action on an appropriate device.
5. Return to Identity or Work to correct the saved content if something reads poorly.

**What happens next:** You have reviewed the public mobile layout and its essential actions.

**Good to know:** Different devices can handle video autoplay and codecs differently. A desktop preview is not a complete device test.

Related: Set separate desktop and mobile banners; Open images, videos and documents as a visitor; Fix an email, social or WhatsApp contact link.

### Open images, videos and documents as a visitor

Understand the public collection and media-viewing experience.

Guide: /help/portfolio?guide=portfolio-media-view

**Before you begin:** The public portfolio link; no creator login is needed for ordinary viewing.

1. Open the public portfolio and navigate to the work collection you want.
2. Select a section or media tile.
3. Use the available enlarged-media or video player controls to inspect the work.
4. Close the viewer to return to the collection and choose another item.
5. Use the configured contact actions if you want to discuss a project with the creator.

**What happens next:** You can explore the showcased work without entering the owner’s editing dashboard.

**Good to know:** Portfolio is a showcase, not the private paid-file delivery portal. Do not expect file approval, client payment confirmation or private delivery download controls here.

Related: Preview and share the public portfolio; Check the portfolio on a phone; Set your email, WhatsApp and call to action.

### Use the canonical link and understand older subdomains

Share the address the current portfolio studio provides.

Guide: /help/portfolio?guide=portfolio-links

**Before you begin:** The portfolio owner or someone given its public link.

1. Open the intended portfolio in the studio and use its current public-preview link.
2. Copy the canonical /portfolio/slug address rather than constructing an address from the display name.
3. If you have an older useshowwork.com portfolio subdomain, test it separately; the application includes legacy-host routing support.
4. Use the canonical link when an older address depends on unresolved DNS or hosting configuration.
5. Report a broken older host to the site administrator with the hostname and visible error, not account credentials.

**What happens next:** You share a current working route and distinguish application routing from domain setup.

**Good to know:** Editing the displayed company name does not automatically change its saved slug. The current editor does not provide a general self-service custom-domain or slug-renaming guide.

Related: Preview and share the public portfolio; Troubleshoot a portfolio link or unavailable studio; Update your name and tagline.

## Client testimonials

Collect, approve and manage genuine client stories.

### Invite a client to leave a testimonial

Collect authentic feedback through the portfolio’s submission link.

Guide: /help/portfolio?guide=collect-testimonials

**Before you begin:** The portfolio owner and a client willing to provide feedback.

1. Open Testimonials in Portfolio Studio.
2. Find and copy the testimonial request/submission link shown for the portfolio.
3. Share the link privately with the client and explain that it is for a public-facing testimonial.
4. Ask the client to enter their name, role if applicable, testimonial and optional rating.
5. Review the incoming submission in Testimonials before deciding whether to publish it.

**What happens next:** The client submission arrives for your review.

**Good to know:** Publicly submitted testimonials are created awaiting approval. Do not assume submission alone makes a quote visible on the portfolio.

Related: Submit a testimonial as a client; Approve, hide or delete testimonials; Add an existing client testimonial.

### Submit a testimonial as a client

Tell a creator what it was like to work together.

Guide: /help/portfolio?guide=submit-testimonial

**Before you begin:** The testimonial submission link supplied by the creator.

1. Open the testimonial link for the intended portfolio.
2. Enter your name and the testimonial text; include your role if helpful.
3. Choose an optional rating using the provided controls.
4. Submit and wait for confirmation.
5. Contact the creator if you need to correct submitted information rather than repeatedly submitting duplicates.

**What happens next:** The testimonial is submitted for the portfolio owner’s approval.

**Good to know:** Submission does not guarantee publication. Share only information you are comfortable having displayed publicly with your feedback.

Related: Invite a client to leave a testimonial; Approve, hide or delete testimonials.

### Add an existing client testimonial

Record feedback you already have permission to use.

Guide: /help/portfolio?guide=add-testimonial

**Before you begin:** The portfolio owner.

1. Open Testimonials and select Add testimonial.
2. Enter the client’s name and role where appropriate.
3. Enter the actual quote and optional rating using the provided fields.
4. Submit the form and check the resulting visibility state.
5. Open the public preview to confirm only the intended testimonial is shown.

**What happens next:** The testimonial is stored in the portfolio’s testimonial collection.

**Good to know:** Owner-added testimonials may already be published by default. Check the status immediately and use Hide if necessary. Do not invent quotes or imply an endorsement the client has not authorized.

Related: Approve, hide or delete testimonials; Invite a client to leave a testimonial.

### Approve, hide or delete testimonials

Control which client stories appear publicly.

Guide: /help/portfolio?guide=manage-testimonials

**Before you begin:** The portfolio owner.

1. Open Testimonials and inspect entries waiting for approval and those already published.
2. Read the name, role, quote and rating before choosing Approve.
3. Use Hide on a published entry if it should remain saved but no longer appear publicly.
4. Use Delete only when you intend to remove the testimonial record.
5. Check the public page after the action completes.

**What happens next:** The public testimonial section reflects the entries currently approved for display.

**Good to know:** Hiding and deleting are different actions. Removing a quote from public view does not rewrite other copies or messages already shared.

Related: Invite a client to leave a testimonial; Add an existing client testimonial; Find a testimonial that is not appearing publicly.

## Troubleshooting

Resolve common upload, preview and sharing questions.

### Recover from a failed portfolio upload

Inspect saved work and reuse the uploader’s available recovery controls.

Guide: /help/portfolio?guide=portfolio-upload-retry

**Before you begin:** The portfolio owner with an upload in progress or an existing section.

1. Read the transfer, preview or finalization error shown by the uploader.
2. Keep the upload page open and use the offered retry/resume controls when available.
3. Check Work for files that were already saved before selecting the same batch again.
4. Reselect the local files if the uploader asks, and verify you are using the same intended section.
5. After the operation completes, preview the collection and confirm the result.

**What happens next:** You recover the unsuccessful part of the upload without intentionally duplicating successful work.

**Good to know:** Large-file resume depends on valid session state and file identity. A browser reload cannot be assumed to preserve every form field or local file selection.

Related: Add files to an existing section; Understand video thumbnails and playback; Troubleshoot slow thumbnails or video playback.

### Troubleshoot slow thumbnails or video playback

Distinguish a missing poster from an incomplete upload or incompatible video.

Guide: /help/portfolio?guide=portfolio-preview-problems

**Before you begin:** The portfolio owner or public visitor.

1. Check the connection and allow the selected media to load.
2. For video, inspect whether the original plays even if a thumbnail is missing.
3. Read any preview-generation warning from the upload; do not assume it means the original transfer failed.
4. Try a current browser that supports the file’s codec, or prepare a compatible public showcase copy.
5. Ask the site administrator about older videos without saved posters instead of repeatedly re-uploading a large archive.

**What happens next:** You can identify whether the issue is media transfer, poster generation, device support or network speed.

**Good to know:** New poster generation does not automatically update every old video. Identity banner uploads and gallery posters are separate paths.

Related: Understand video thumbnails and playback; Choose reviewable image, video and document files; Recover from a failed portfolio upload.

### Troubleshoot changes missing from the public page

Check saving, portfolio identity and visibility before repeating an edit.

Guide: /help/portfolio?guide=portfolio-changes-missing

**Before you begin:** The portfolio owner.

1. Return to the editor and confirm the relevant Save changes or upload operation completed.
2. Open the public link from the studio rather than an old copied address.
3. Check that you are viewing the intended portfolio, especially in an agency account.
4. For a missing testimonial, check its approval state; for a section, confirm it contains saved media.
5. If the saved result still differs, note the section and action and contact support before making repeated replacement edits.

**What happens next:** You narrow down whether the issue is an unsaved edit, wrong portfolio, empty section or visibility setting.

Related: Understand when saved changes appear publicly; Approve, hide or delete testimonials; Choose the correct portfolio in an agency collection.

Related: Invite a client to leave a testimonial; Approve, hide or delete testimonials; Troubleshoot changes missing from the public page.

### Fix an email, social or WhatsApp contact link

Verify the address visitors actually open.

Guide: /help/portfolio?guide=portfolio-contact-problems

**Before you begin:** The portfolio owner.

1. Open Identity and inspect the saved contact or social field.
2. Check the email spelling, international WhatsApp number or full intended social-profile address.
3. Save the corrected value and wait for confirmation.
4. Test the action from the public portfolio on a device with the relevant app or email handler.
5. If the link is correct but the app does not open, inspect the device’s handler/app settings rather than repeatedly changing the saved profile.

**What happens next:** The public action points to the intended contact destination.

**Good to know:** A WhatsApp contact widget opens a contact flow; it does not guarantee that a visitor sent a message or that the creator received it.

### Troubleshoot a portfolio link or unavailable studio

Check the canonical address, owner account and relevant access state.

Guide: /help/portfolio?guide=portfolio-access-problems

**Before you begin:** The public link for visitors; owner login for studio access.

1. For public viewing, use the /portfolio/slug link copied from the studio.
2. If an older subdomain fails, test the canonical link and ask the administrator to inspect the old host’s DNS/routing.
3. For editing, sign in to the account that owns the portfolio and open it from the dashboard.
4. For an agency portfolio, inspect any billing/payment-required notice and follow the relevant account flow.
5. If the route still fails, record the exact address and visible error for support without sharing passwords or private tokens.

**What happens next:** You distinguish a broken legacy host from an incorrect account, missing record or access requirement.

**Good to know:** The public portfolio does not have a delivery-style client access code. Do not ask visitors for your Showwork login to view it.

Related: Use the canonical link and understand older subdomains; Complete payment for an agency portfolio; Preview and share the public portfolio.
