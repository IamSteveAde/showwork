# Showwork messaging guide

## Core message

Showwork helps creators and agencies build portfolios, deliver projects and manage client accounts.

Use this fuller explanation when space allows:

> Build a portfolio, deliver projects and manage client content, approvals, conversations, leads and analytics. Showwork is built for creators, agencies and social media teams.

## Product names and audiences

- **Portfolio**: a professional place to show projects, services and testimonials and help potential clients discover your work.
- **Project Delivery**: a branded client page for project files, feedback, approvals and final downloads. Describe this as a project delivery page to distinguish it from Content Workspace.
- **Content Workspace**: the product and subscription name. Each client gets a dedicated **client workspace** for content, business knowledge, approvals, publishing, conversations, leads and analytics. Built for social media managers, agencies and teams managing ongoing accounts.
- **Creativo**: the creator community. Keep community benefits distinct from software features.

## Explain the connected workflow

1. Add the client’s business knowledge and invite the team.
2. Plan content manually or generate ideas, drafts and calendars with AI.
3. Let clients preview content, leave feedback, request revisions and approve posts.
4. Publish approved content to supported connected channels.
5. Manage conversations in Inbox and organize contacts and follow-up in Leads.
6. Explore performance and lead opportunities in Analytics & Reporting, then use AI recommendations for the next content batch.

## Accurate claims

- Publishing, messaging and reporting depend on the platform, account connection and granted permissions. Avoid claiming every connected network supports every feature.
- WhatsApp Business supports conversations; do not imply it offers the same publishing and analytics as social channels.
- AI customer care can prepare replies from business knowledge. Automatic replies are available for supported channels; avoid implying universal automatic messaging.
- Lead management includes manual contacts, CSV import/export, social contacts, notes, temperature and pipeline status. Avoid claiming automatic sales conversion.
- Analytics includes supported account and post metrics, date/platform filters, lead opportunities and AI recommendations. Avoid guaranteeing growth, revenue or engagement.
- AI drafts and recommendations support the team’s decisions. Avoid promising perfect content or guaranteed results.
- Account creation, a free portfolio and free project delivery are distinct from the 7-day Content Workspace trial.
- Workspace plans are account subscriptions with active-workspace limits, not a universal per-client fee. Creator starts at ₦4,900/month for 1 active client workspace; Studio costs ₦29,900/month and supports up to 10. Unlimited costs ₦59,900/month with unlimited workspaces and collaborators, 200 GB storage, and 2,000 AI generations per month. Verify prices and allowances against `lib/contentWorkspaceEntitlements.ts` when changing pricing copy.

## Voice

Use clear, direct language: what someone can do, where they do it and why it helps. Prefer concrete actions such as “collect approvals,” “follow up with leads” and “compare post performance” to vague promises about premium positioning or managing everything. Keep headlines short and support them with a specific explanation. Use the same product names in navigation, landing pages, onboarding, billing and emails.
